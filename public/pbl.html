1 — High-Level Summary

BHPs core mission is to eliminate manual data entry and queue delays for hospital intake and billing by issuing a tokenized digital health access card (QR) from a single mobile app.

"QR = authorization to defer settlement + pointer to evidence & liability."

Everything else is automated: OCR → template mapping → RPA/API submission → tracking → settle / fallback.

4 — Core Design Decisions

Single App (Two Modes)

Strict device registration + RBAC. Simpler distribution, unified UX, less dev overhead.

Deferred Settlement

Platform authorizes treatment via exposure caps; backend handles verification later. Enables no-queue care.

RPA + Email Fallbacks

Simulates human submission when APIs are unavailable. Allows scale without privileged integrations.

3 — End-to-End Workflow

01

Onboarding (Patient)

  • Phone + OTP verification via Firebase.
  • Doc upload: ID, Policy, Selfie for matching.
  • Backend issues signed, time-bound QR token.
02

Visit + Authorization

  • Hospital scans QR → Real-time scan endpoint.
  • Identity & Risk agents compute exposure cap.
  • Authorization issued; Zero-Queue treatment starts.
03

Claims Automation

  • Treatment complete → Invoice OCR extraction.
  • RPA Engine submits to insurer portal/portal stubs.
  • Automated status tracking & human-in-loop handling.
04

Settlement & Audit

  • Released payment upon claim approval.
  • Signed event logs & evidence bundle archival.
  • Fallback recovery if claim rejected after timeout.

8 — Claims Automation

OCR / Parser

Multi-stage pipeline: Normalization → Tesseract/Azure OCR → Custom NER Model. Extracts line items, provider details, and totals with per-field confidence.

Template Engine

Rule-based mapping from extracted JSON to insurer-specific fields. Identifies missing ICD codes or rejection heuristics before submission.

RPA Connectors

Headless Puppeteer pool simulates human navigation on insurer portals. Manages auth sessions, form filling, and evidence uploads autonomously.

11 — Security & Privacy

  • • Device Binding: HMAC signed tokens bound to device certs.
  • • Anti-Replay: Single-use nonces for every scanning event.
  • • PII Minimal: No raw Aadhaar storage; fully masked identities.
  • • Audit Logs: HMAC-signed append-only event streams.

16 — Legal
